✦ NFC Guide ✦

Write Your Own NFC Tag

Personalize your physical art with digital treasures. Follow this step-by-step guide to program your tags.

download

Recommended App: NFC Tools

For the best experience, use the NFC Tools app — available on iOS and Android. It offers a user-friendly interface for all types of NFC records.

✦ Steps ✦
1
auto_fix_normal

Erase the Tag

Open NFC Tools, go to the Other tab, and select Erase Tag. Tap the tag to your phone sensor to ensure it's fresh and ready.

2
add_circle

Add a Record

Tap the Write tab, then Add a record. Choose 'URL' to link a website, or 'Social Network' for your profiles. Enter your information and click OK.

3
sensors

Write to Tag

Press the Write button and place the NFC tag to your phone. Hold it until you see the 'Write Complete!' success checkmark.
